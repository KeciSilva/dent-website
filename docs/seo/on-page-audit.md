# On-Page SEO Audit

| Page Type | Before | After | Expected Impact |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Homepage | Single-language clinic page without locale routing. | `/sq` and `/en` localized homepages with one H1, service links and CTA. | Clearer search targeting and better UX for Albanian and English users. |
| Services | Services were not fully bilingual in locale URLs. | Service overview plus 7 individual service pages per locale. | Stronger topical targeting for treatment searches. |
| Service pages | Limited landing-page depth. | Each service has intro, audience, process, FAQ, related services and CTA. | Better relevance for high-intent dental queries. |
| Blog | Basic blog structure. | 4 localized educational articles with internal links. | Supports topical authority and patient education. |
| Contact | Contact details were not in a bilingual local SEO structure. | Crawlable NAP, tel/mailto links, hours and map iframe. | Improved local intent and conversion clarity. |
| Metadata | Mixed single-language metadata. | Localized titles/descriptions, canonical and hreflang alternates. | Better indexation signals for both languages. |
